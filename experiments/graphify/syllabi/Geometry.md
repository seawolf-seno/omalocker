# Geometry: Course Syllabus

Teacher: Ms. Haddad (fictional) · Room M118 · Block 1B

## How this course works
In geometry nothing is true because it looks true. We start from what we are given,
apply definitions and theorems one step at a time, and reach a conclusion no one can
reasonably deny. Every proof is a chain of reasons; a missing link means no proof.

## Unit 1: Points, lines and angles
Definitions, postulates, angle relationships, parallel lines cut by a transversal.

## Unit 2: Writing proofs
Two-column and paragraph proofs, proof by contradiction, and finding the counterexample
that breaks a false conjecture.

## Unit 3: Transformations and symmetry
Reflections, rotations, translations and dilations. Line symmetry and rotational
symmetry; tessellations; the golden ratio and why it shows up in so many places.

## Unit 4: Right triangles and trigonometry
The Pythagorean theorem, special right triangles, sine, cosine and tangent, and using
them to find heights we cannot measure directly.

## Assessment
Mastery of each learning target: Not yet, Beginning, Developing, Proficient, Extending.
