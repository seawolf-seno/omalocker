# Theology 10: Course Syllabus

Teacher: Br. Castellan (fictional) · Room C002 · Block 3B

## How this course works
Faith and reason work together. We read sources carefully, state what we believe and
why, and answer serious objections honestly. A good answer names its sources and shows
how they lead to its conclusion.

## Unit 1: Creation and our place in it
The creation accounts in Genesis, the goodness of the created world, and the human task
of tending the garden. Students study one local environmental problem and write what
care for creation asks of them, citing both Scripture and the facts of the case.

## Unit 2: Many members, one body
Paul's image of the community as a body: each part needs the others, and when one part
suffers, all suffer. How communities hold together, how they correct themselves, and what
happens when they don't.

## Unit 3: The sacraments
The seven sacraments, their signs and their history.

## Unit 4: Making the case
Classic arguments about God, the problem of evil, and how to answer an objection without
dismissing the person who raises it. Students write a short apologetic essay.

## Assessment
Mastery of each learning target: Not yet, Beginning, Developing, Proficient, Extending.
