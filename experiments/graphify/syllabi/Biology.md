# Biology (Grade 10): Course Syllabus

Teacher: Mr. Okafor (fictional) · Room S104 · Block 2A

## How this course works
We learn biology the way biologists do it: we ask a question, collect data, and decide
what the data lets us claim. Every lab report ends with a claim, the data that backs it,
and the reasoning that connects the two. A claim the data can't support is sent back.

## Unit 1: Cells and how they divide
Cell structure, the cell cycle, mitosis and meiosis. We grow onion root tips and count
cells in each phase to estimate how long each phase lasts.

## Unit 2: Staying in balance
Bodies hold temperature, blood sugar and water within narrow limits. When a value drifts,
sensors notice and the body pushes it back: insulin and glucagon, sweating and shivering.
We model these loops on paper and ask what happens when one part fails, as in diabetes.

## Unit 3: Ecosystems and food webs
Energy flows from the sun through producers to consumers. Predator and prey numbers rise
and fall together: more rabbits feed more foxes, more foxes mean fewer rabbits. We graph
real population data and find the loop in it.

## Unit 4: Caring for living systems
Habitat loss, invasive species and restoration. Each student adopts a local creek or park,
documents what lives there, and proposes one change that would help it, with evidence.

## Unit 5: Shape and symmetry in living things
Why most animals have a left and a right side, why starfish come in fives, why sunflower
seeds spiral. We measure leaves and shells and look for repeating patterns and ratios.

## Assessment
Mastery of each learning target: Not yet, Beginning, Developing, Proficient, Extending.
