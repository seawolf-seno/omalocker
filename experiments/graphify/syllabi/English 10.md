# English 10: Course Syllabus

Teacher: Ms. Lindqvist (fictional) · Room H210 · Block 4A

## How this course works
We read closely and write to persuade. Every essay makes a claim, supports it with
quotations from the text, and explains how each quotation proves the point. "Because I
felt it" is not a reason; "because line 14 says" is the start of one.

## Unit 1: Narrative voice
Who is telling the story, and can we trust them? First person, third person limited,
unreliable narrators. Students rewrite one scene from another character's point of view.

## Unit 2: The argumentative essay
Claim, evidence, warrant, counterclaim, rebuttal. Students take a position on a school
policy and defend it against the strongest objection a classmate can raise.

## Unit 3: Form in poetry
Meter, rhyme scheme, the sonnet's fourteen lines and its turn. How a poem's repeating
structure, and the places it breaks that structure, carry its meaning. Students write a
poem that keeps a strict form for twelve lines and then breaks it on purpose.

## Unit 4: Writing about the natural world
Nature writing from the Psalms to modern essays about rivers and forests. What do we owe
the places we write about? Students write a personal essay about a place they want to
protect.

## Assessment
Mastery of each learning target: Not yet, Beginning, Developing, Proficient, Extending.
